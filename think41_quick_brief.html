Marketing Quick Brief · June 2026

Think41 at a Glance

World-class product. Near-zero market visibility. Think41 ships real, production-grade GenAI — ExtraSuite publicly launched March 2026, Foundation Voice open-sourced June 2025, Mindlap in active beta, Dev41 live with Fortune 500 clients. The founders carry serious credibility: they built and sold HashedIn to Deloitte, then exited to build Think41 with the same team. But Think41's website is a client-rendered SPA with 0 indexable pages, no content engine, no founder visibility online, and no brand presence commensurate with what it's actually building. The gap between what exists and what the world sees is the entire marketing opportunity.

Think41's 4 Products

Launched publicly March 17, 2026

ExtraSuite

LIVE · OPEN SOURCE
Open-source AI execution layer for Google Workspace. Lets AI agents (Claude, Codex, Gemini) safely operate inside Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, Apps Script — with governance built in. Service-account-per-user identity, short-lived scoped tokens, full audit trail via Google version history. 100+ team members run 100+ workflows on it internally.
"Many AI projects fail at the same point: the model can generate content, but cannot reliably write it back into the system of record. We built ExtraSuite so agents can focus on the task while execution, controls, and auditability stay consistent." — Sripathi Krishnan

Think41's SWOT

Strengths
  • Founders with proven exit: HashedIn → Deloitte acquisition
  • Dual model: services revenue funds product R&D
  • 2 open-source products build community credibility
  • Fortune 500 client validation before any marketing spend
  • 100+ team eating own dog food with ExtraSuite
  • Unfunded — no investor pressure, founder-controlled
Weaknesses
  • think41.com = 0 indexed pages (SPA, invisible to Google)
  • Founders have near-zero public LinkedIn/social presence
  • No content engine, no editorial calendar, no blog
  • No analyst relationships (Gartner, Forrester, IDC)
  • Product brands not connected to Think41 parent brand
  • No dedicated marketing function yet
Opportunities
  • Enterprise AI adoption wave — 18-month first-mover window
  • Open-source community = zero-cost inbound pipeline
  • Founder story is genuinely differentiated and underused
  • Hacker News, GitHub, dev communities not yet tapped
  • BFSI, SaaS, manufacturing verticals actively seeking partners
  • India's open-source movement gaining global attention
Threats
  • Microsoft, Google, Salesforce entering same enterprise AI layer
  • Competitors moving faster on content, SEO, and community
  • "AI fatigue" — noise drowning real-signal vendors
  • Talent war: engineers who can also communicate externally
  • Open-source forks could dilute product differentiation
Priority Issues

7 Marketing Gaps — Ranked by Severity

think41.com is a ghost to Google
The site is a Vite/React SPA — every page renders client-side, so crawlers see nothing. Fix: rebuild with Next.js/Gatsby SSG or add a static layer. Target: 50+ indexed pages in 90 days.
Critical
Founders are invisible online
Four credible, story-rich founders with zero regular LinkedIn presence. Fix: ghost-write if needed, 2 posts/week per founder. The HashedIn → Deloitte → Think41 arc is a compelling narrative that sells itself.
Critical
No content engine exists
No blog. No newsletter. No editorial calendar. Think41 ships products and sends press releases — but creates no ongoing content that builds authority or drives inbound. Fix: 4 blog posts/month + repurpose into LinkedIn + newsletter.
Critical
Open-source community not activated
ExtraSuite and Foundation Voice are on GitHub but have no community layer. No Discord, no GitHub Discussions, no "Good First Issue" labels, no Show HN posts. Fix: community setup + Show HN launch for both repos.
Critical
No media or analyst relationships
Press releases go on Business Standard but no ongoing journalist or analyst relationships. Fix: identify 5 India tech journalists at ET, Inc42, YourStory. Brief them quarterly. Target 2 earned features in 6 months.
Significant
Product names don't connect to Think41 brand
ExtraSuite, Foundation Voice, Mindlap, Dev41 — four products, four visual identities, no brand architecture tying them to Think41. Fix: "ExtraSuite by Think41" framing + unified design system.
Significant
Zero events and conference presence
No speaking slots, no booths, no panels. Fix: target SaaS Insider India, NASSCOM, Bengaluru Tech Summit, Google Next India. Speak — don't just exhibit.

Founder Visibility

What Think41's Founders Should (and Shouldn't) Do Online

✓ Do
  • Post real opinions on AI/LLMs — even contrarian ones
  • Document what they're building and why in real-time
  • Share what didn't work — authenticity builds trust faster than polish
  • Speak at SaaS Insider, NASSCOM, Bengaluru tech meetups
  • Share customer outcomes with permission (even anonymised)
  • Engage on comments — even 1 sentence reply builds community
✗ Don't
  • Post only product announcements and press releases
  • Use generic AI-generated LinkedIn content
  • Go silent for months between posts (consistency > virality)
  • Avoid taking positioning stands — "we're different" needs proof
  • Leave comments unanswered — signals disengagement
  • Over-invest in Twitter before LinkedIn is consistent
